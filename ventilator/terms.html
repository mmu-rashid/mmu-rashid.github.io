Purpose of the App

The App is an educational tool for exploring mechanical ventilation. It lets you adjust simulated ventilator and patient settings, observe waveforms and regional ventilation, and practice recognizing patterns in training scenarios.

Educational Use Only

The App generates simulated data, including its electrical impedance tomography (EIT) displays and alarms. Use it for learning and teaching.
