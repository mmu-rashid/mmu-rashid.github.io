Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No. The simulator runs offline without sign-in. Your settings, lesson progress, and saved scenarios are stored on your device.

What can I do with the simulator?

You can adjust simulated ventilator and patient settings, explore waveforms and regional ventilation, follow guided lessons, and practice recognizing patient–ventilator asynchrony. The simulator is designed for education and training.

Can I save or share a training scenario?

Yes. Use the saved scenarios controls to save a setup or export scenarios as a JSON file. You can import a scenario JSON file from the same screen.

How do I delete saved scenarios or reset local data?

Use the Delete button next to a saved scenario to remove it. You can also clear leaderboard entries from the Shoot Asynchrony game screen. To remove all local app data on iPhone or iPad, choose Delete App. If you use the browser version, clear the simulator’s site data in your browser. Exported files are managed separately wherever you saved them.
