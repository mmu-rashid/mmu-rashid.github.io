Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ventilator Simulator Pro (“the App”), developed by Dr. Md Harun Ur Rashid (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use the App.

Summary

The App is an educational tool for learning mechanical ventilation. It runs offline and does not require an account. Simulation calculations, training progress, and settings stay on your device. You choose whether to export and share any of this information.

Information We Collect

Data stored only on your device

The App calculates simulated waveforms, measurements, and regional ventilation on your device. It uses local storage to remember:

  • Appearance preferences, alarm limits, and sound settings.
  • Lesson progress, game scores, and optional names entered on the local leaderboard.
  • Custom training cases, simulated patient profiles, and saved scenarios, including any names, objectives, or notes you enter.

Use fictional or non-identifying details in training cases, notes, and imported scenarios.

Analytics and crash reports

The App has no analytics or crash reporting service and does not send us usage data or crash reports.

Simulator alarms

Simulator alarms and sounds are generated on your device during training. The App does not send push notifications.

Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use this information to respond and follow up on your request. Please leave patient information out of support emails.

Advertising

The App does not display ads, use advertising identifiers, or track you across apps or websites.

Children’s Privacy

The App is designed for people learning or teaching mechanical ventilation. It has no registration form and does not ask for your age. If you believe a child sent us personal information by email, contact us to request its deletion.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising or marketing.

The App reads scenario files you select on your device. You can export scenario files, session reports, and trend data. These files may contain details you entered or results the App generated. If you choose to save or share an export through another app or service, that app or service handles its copy under its own privacy practices.

If you download the App from the App Store, Apple handles the download and processes App Store information separately. See Apple’s App Store privacy notice for details.

Your Choices

Saved information can remain on your device between sessions. You can delete individual scenarios, custom cases, and simulated patient profiles in the App, and clear leaderboard entries from the game screen. To remove the App’s local data on iPhone or iPad, choose Delete App. If you use the browser version, clear the simulator’s site data in your browser. You can delete exported copies separately from wherever you saved them.

You can use the contact address below to ask about or request deletion of support correspondence.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices change. We will post the revised policy here with a new last updated date.
